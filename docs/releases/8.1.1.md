## Microsoft Decision-1 in Enabled models

- Show available SystemOne decision models, including `microsoft-decision-1`, in **Settings → Models → Enabled models** even when Copilot hides them from its chat picker.
- Give decision models their own **Decisions** label, `/v1/systemone` endpoint, and enable switch. The saved switch is honored by SystemOne requests and survives refresh and restart.
- Keep existing chat-model rows, context displays, reasoning labels, and switches unchanged.
- Keep decision models out of Codex's generated chat catalog, Claude role selectors, and the provider card's Codex model count. A decision model cannot be used as a chat model merely because its switch is on.

After updating, use **Refresh models** if Decision-1 is not already shown. Availability still depends on the selected Copilot account.
